04 / Standards

Community Guidelines

WAIN is designed to help ERLC communities run organised sessions. Everyone using the system should help maintain a respectful and dependable environment.

01

Respect others

No harassment, threats, hate speech, targeted abuse or discriminatory conduct.

02

Be trustworthy

Do not impersonate staff, falsify records, abuse permissions or misrepresent activity.

03

Protect access

Keep credentials private. Never attempt to access another user's account or data.

04

Use tools properly

Do not spam, exploit bugs, disrupt service or use management tools outside your authority.

Violations may result in warnings, permission changes, temporary suspension or permanent removal, depending on severity and context. Provide an appeal route at [appeals contact or process].
